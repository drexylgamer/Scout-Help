Things you should take on an overnight tramp:

  • Tent
  • Sleeping Bag/Liner
  • Sleeping Mat
  • Hiking Pack
  • Warm Clothes (Multiple Pairs)
  • Thermals
  • Communication (Phone, PLB)
  • Gas Cooker
  • Pot/Pan
  • Water Bottles
  • Dehydrated Food
  • Snacks
  • Sunscreen, a sun hat, and sunglasses.
  • Insect repellent
  • Toilet Paper
  • Torch
  • First Aid Kit
  • Rain Jacket
  • Tramping Boots/Socks
  • Spare Shoes
  • Foldable Towel
  • Track Pant/Shorts
